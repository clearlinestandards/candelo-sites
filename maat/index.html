Questions owners ask

Does it sound like a robot?
No. Maat uses natural, human-sounding voice and conversation — it greets callers, answers their questions, and books them like a sharp front-desk lead. Most callers just feel like they reached a helpful receptionist.
Will callers know it's AI?
Yes — Maat discloses that the call may use AI-generated voice technology. That's required by law in California and elsewhere, and we think honesty is the right call anyway. It hasn't been a problem; callers care that they got helped, fast.
How long does setup take?
About 24 hours. We learn your business from your website, build and test your receptionist, and have it live the next day. You don't configure anything yourself.
What happens if it can't answer something?
It takes a clear message and routes urgent or sensitive calls straight to you — by transfer or instant text — so a real human picks up exactly where it matters.
Can I keep my current phone number?
Yes. Maat works with your existing number — callers dial the same line they always have. Nothing on your customers' end changes.
Am I locked into a contract?
No long-term lock-in. The Founding offer is $0 setup and $397/mo for your first three months, then your trade's standard monthly rate. Month to month.
